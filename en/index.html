Who we are

About us

Sit & Care is a family-run service based in Walldorf. Together as parents and children we reliably and wholeheartedly look after homes, pets, plants, and the loving care of children in our neighbourhood.

Our story

Why we do this

Our children want to build something of their own and take on real responsibility. With Sit & Care we combine the useful with the meaningful: we support neighbours and families in Walldorf and the surrounding area — and the kids can earn pocket money to fund their dream of studying abroad.

What we offer

Our Services

Service area: Walldorf and surroundings (5 km)

House Sitting

Regular check-ins, airing rooms and collecting post — so your home stays cared for while you're away.

Pet Care

Feeding, walking and playing — your pets are in the best of hands with us.

Plant Care

Watering indoor and balcony plants — so your greenery thrives even when you're not there.

Babysitting

Loving care for your children for short or longer periods — reliable and warm-hearted.

Key Handling

Safe handover and responsible keeping of your keys.
